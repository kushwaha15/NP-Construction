What We Offer

Structural Steel Services

From TMT rebar work to complete industrial steel structures — we handle it all.

TMT Bar & Rebar Work

We provide expert TMT bar bending, cutting, and placement for all RCC structural elements including columns, beams, slabs, shear walls, and foundations. Our team follows IS 456 specifications for bar diameter, spacing, cover, and lap lengths.

  • Column reinforcement cages
  • Beam and slab rebar placement
  • Foundation and pile cap steel
  • Stirrup and link fabrication

Structural Steel Fabrication

We fabricate custom structural steel components at our yard or on-site, based on structural drawings. All fabrication work follows IS 800 standards with quality checks at every stage.

  • I-beams, H-columns, channels, angles
  • Gusset plates, base plates, cleats
  • Custom brackets and connections
  • Staircase & mezzanine steel

Steel Framework Erection

Our experienced erection teams use cranes, chain pulleys, and safety rigging to assemble and erect steel frameworks safely and efficiently, meeting all project timelines.

  • Multi-storey steel frame buildings
  • Portal frame structures
  • Mezzanine floor installation
  • Pre-engineered building (PEB) erection

Welding & Fabrication

Our certified welders execute MIG, TIG, and SMAW (arc) welding for all structural connections, ensuring full-penetration welds, proper NDT-tested joints, and long-term structural integrity.

  • Structural welding (MIG / TIG / Arc)
  • Weld testing and inspection
  • Repair and reinforcement welding
  • Pipe and hollow section welding

Steel Roof Trusses

We design and fabricate steel roof trusses for long-span roofing applications in factories, warehouses, stadiums, and commercial buildings where traditional RCC is not feasible.

  • Pratt, Howe, and Warren trusses
  • Hip and valley truss systems
  • Purlin and rafter installation
  • Roofing sheet support systems

Industrial Steel Structures

Complete structural steel solutions for warehouses, manufacturing plants, cold storage, logistics hubs, and industrial facilities — from design coordination to turnkey erection.

  • Warehouse & factory structures
  • Storage racks and platforms
  • Crane girder fabrication
  • Overhead equipment support frames
