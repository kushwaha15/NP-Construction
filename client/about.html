Our Story

Built from the Ground Up

NP Construction was founded with a simple vision: to bring world-class structural steel contracting to every corner of India. Starting as a small team of dedicated iron workers in 2010, we have grown into one of the most trusted names in the structural steel industry.

Over the years, we've worked on everything from small residential buildings to large-scale commercial complexes and industrial warehouses. Our commitment to quality, safety, and timely delivery has earned us long-term relationships with leading developers and builders.

We employ IS-code-trained engineers, certified welders, and experienced bar-benders who work together to deliver precision on every project — whether it's 50 tons or 5,000 tons of steel.
